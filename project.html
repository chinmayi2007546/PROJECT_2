AIM

Objective of the experiment

Experiment Objective

The aim of this virtual lab experiment is to understand the concept of Linear Differential Equations (LDE) including the formation and use of the Auxiliary Equation and methods for obtaining the general solution through interactive tools and visual demonstrations.

By the end of this experiment, students will be able to:

  • Define and explain Linear Differential Equations with constant coefficients.
  • Form the Auxiliary Equation corresponding to a given differential equation.
  • Find the roots of the Auxiliary Equation and interpret their nature.
  • Obtain the complementary function (Yc) and particular integral (Yp).
  • Apply LDE concepts to solve practical mathematical and engineering problems.

Theory

Background concepts & definitions

What is a Linear Differential Equation (LDE)?

ALinear Differential Equation (LDE) is an equation involving derivatives of a dependent variable where the variable and its derivatives appear linearly. It is widely used to model physical systems and engineering problems.

Auxiliary Equation (A.E.)

The Auxiliary Equation is formed by replacing:

  • dny / dxn → mn
  • dn-1y / dxn-1 → mn-1

This converts the differential equation into an algebraic equation used to find the roots.

m^2+5*m+6=0

Complimentary Solution (Yc)

The Complementary Function (C.F.) or Yc is the solution obtained from the auxiliary equation.

Yc=C1e^(-2x)+C2e^(-3x)

The Particular Solution (I.P) or Yp is the specific solution corresponding to the non-homogeneous term f(x).

Y=Yc+Yp

Practical Applications

  • Electrical circuit analysis
  • Mechanical vibration systems
  • Population growth models
  • Heat transfer and wave motion

Key Properties

  • The general solution is the sum of complementary and particular solutions.
  • The nature of roots of the auxiliary equation determines the form of Yc.
  • Higher-order LDEs can be solved systematically using operator methods.
  • LDEs help model real-world engineering and scientific systems.
